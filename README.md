## Currently working on
Bayesian marketing-mix modeling with PyMC and training-free probabilistic time-series forecasting methods.

## Focus areas
- Retail and customer analytics: market basket analysis, customer decision trees, product behavioral taxonomy, PCA-based clustering
- Time-series forecasting: conformal seasonal pools, AutoMFLES, NeuralProphet
- Marketing measurement: Bayesian marketing-mix modeling with PyMC
- Applied ML: multi-class and multi-output prediction, graph-based models, classification on medical imaging data

## Featured projects
- [market_basket_app](https://github.com/hrczggyrgy/market_basket_app) - Streamlit app for market basket analysis and customer segmentation; decision-tree-based product taxonomy for retail decision support
- [Neural_customer_decision_graph](https://github.com/hrczggyrgy/Neural_customer_decision_graph) - Graph neural network approach to market basket analysis, modeling product co-purchase relationships beyond pairwise association rules
- [customer_decision_tree_v2](https://github.com/hrczggyrgy/customer_decision_tree_v2) - Retail analytics pipeline combining product behavioral taxonomy, PCA, and balanced clustering to segment customer decision patterns
- [pymc_MMM_workbench](https://github.com/hrczggyrgy/pymc_MMM_workbench) - Bayesian marketing-mix modeling workbench built with PyMC and deployed as a Streamlit app
- [nixtla_conformalseasonalpool](https://github.com/hrczggyrgy/nixtla_conformalseasonalpool) - Training-free probabilistic forecaster producing calibrated seasonal naive prediction intervals without parameter estimation
- [Xray-Classifier-Project](https://github.com/hrczggyrgy/Xray-Classifier-Project) - Deep learning classifier distinguishing pneumonia from normal chest X-rays, built with PyTorch and TensorFlow
